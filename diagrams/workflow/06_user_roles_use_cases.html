📝 How to Draw and Explain User Roles & Use Cases to Your Supervisor:

1. Five Actors (4 Human + 1 System) Explain: "Sir, we have 4 human actors (Floor Operator, Quality Inspector, Quality Manager, System Admin) plus 1 autonomous system actor: the Hardware Stand itself, which monitors lux and counts pulses."
2. Clear Boundary of Responsibilities Explain: "The Floor Operator uses the mobile app to scan rolls and acknowledge urgent defect alarms. The Quality Inspector reviews defect crops on the web dashboard to correct false positives."
3. Manager & Certificate Authority Explain: "Only the Quality Manager has permission to finalize the roll's ASTM D5430 grade and export the official signed PDF certificate."
