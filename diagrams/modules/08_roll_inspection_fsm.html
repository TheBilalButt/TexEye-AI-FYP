Operational Architecture & Invariant Guard Dynamics

1. Start & Sensor Calibration Operator scans roll QR code to zero displacement counter and POST session start. Transition into RUNNING occurs strictly upon sensors_ok.
2. Hardware Safety Invariant Fault If lux < 800 or lens blur exceeds threshold, trips into UNINSPECTED_FAULT, actuating physical amber stack light & logging span [Y1, Y2].
3. Critical Defect & Terminal State Any critical flaw (hole > 25mm) transitions to ALARM_ACTIVE, actuating red stack light & siren, requiring operator acknowledgment before finish.
