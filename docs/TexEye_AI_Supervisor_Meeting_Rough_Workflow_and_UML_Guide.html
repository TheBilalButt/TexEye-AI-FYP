3. Sequence Diagram • Module 1: AI Defect Detection & Location Bilal Butt

Operational message flow showing real-time frame ingestion, YOLO bounding box inference, rotary encoder metric mapping, and Kalman filter deduplication.

Supervisor Question: "How do you avoid counting the same defect twice?"
Answer: "Sir, since the camera captures at 30 FPS, a flaw is visible across multiple frames. The Kalman tracker projects the flaw's trajectory along fabric motion and merges matching detections into a single unique defect record."

4. Sequence Diagram • Module 2: Hardware & Inspection Monitoring Bilal Butt

Safety invariant execution trace showing mean lux evaluation, blur detection, physical relay actuation (amber light + siren), and uninspected span logging.

Supervisor Question: "What happens if factory lighting flickers or dims?"
Answer: "Sir, if lux drops below 800, the Reliability Guard trips the physical amber relay, sounds the siren, and logs interval [Y1, Y2] as uninspected in SQLite. It alerts the operator immediately."

5. Sequence Diagram • Module 3: Mobile Floor Application Afnan Khan

Mobile client interaction flow: QR roll scanning, real-time speed & length display, WebSocket haptic vibration alert, and manual floor defect tagging.

Supervisor Question: "Why do floor operators need a mobile app?"
Answer: "Sir, fabric inspection stands are long. The worker walks around the machine. When a defect passes, their phone vibrates via WebSocket push, letting them immediately inspect the physical flaw."

6. Sequence Diagram • Module 4: Web Quality Management & Digital Fabric Map Afnan Khan

Web dashboard flow: HTML5 canvas 2D roll defect map, high-res crop gallery zoom, electronic calipers measurement, defect reclassification, and audit logging.

Supervisor Question: "Can the inspector correct an AI classification error?"
Answer: "Sir, yes! Clicking any defect on the 2D map opens the high-res crop photo. The inspector can measure flaw dimensions with digital calipers and reclassify the defect, creating an immutable audit log."

7. Sequence Diagram • Module 5: Smart Operations & ASTM 4-Point Grading Joint Module

Standard quality grading execution: ASTM D5430-07 penalty calculation (1-4 points, max 4 per meter), area normalization per 100m², roll grade assignment, and SHA-256 PDF signing.

Supervisor Question: "How is ASTM 4-point grading calculated mathematically?"
Answer: "Sir, flaws are assigned 1 to 4 points based on length. Points per 100 square meters are calculated as (Total Points × 10,000) / (Roll Length × Fabric Width). Under 20 points is Grade A; under 40 is Grade B; above 40 is Scrap."
